Our Programmes

We train over 80 children across 3 age divisions. All programmes focus on skills, discipline, and teamwork.

Age Groups

  • U10 Division: Ages 6 - 9. Focus: Basic skills and fun games.
  • U14 Division: Ages 10 - 13. Focus: Teamwork and technique.
  • U18 Division: Ages 14 - 17. Focus: Tactics and competitive play.
