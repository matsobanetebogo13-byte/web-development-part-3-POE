About Us

Our History

Founded in 2015 by former amateur players in Vosloorus, Gauteng. We started to give local children access to structured coaching.

Mission

To develop disciplined, skilled footballers while nurturing character and community spirit.

Vision

To become the leading grassroots football academy in Ekurhuleni, producing players who progress to provincial and professional levels.
